Update the Instawork Research landing page to make the earning value clearer without assuming that the visitor is in New York City.

Do not redesign the entire page. Keep the existing Instawork visual style, typography, colors, navigation, session cards, map, and responsive layout. Focus on copy hierarchy, location selection, dynamic pay information, and conversion flow.

GOAL

The visitor should immediately understand:

1. They can get paid to complete a simple voice recording session.
2. The session takes approximately 3 hours.
3. Pay varies by city and session.
4. They need to select their location to see exact pay and available times.
5. No experience is required.
6. Payment is handled through Instawork.

Do not display New York City as the default location unless the user has already selected it or it is provided by existing page data.

--------------------------------------------------
3. DYNAMIC PAY LOGIC
--------------------------------------------------

Use the available session data for the selected city.

Calculate:

minimumPay = lowest estimated total pay among available sessions
maximumPay = highest estimated total pay among available sessions

Display logic:

- If minimumPay equals maximumPay:
  Show “Get paid $[amount] to record your voice”

- If minimumPay and maximumPay are different:
  Show “Earn $[minimumPay]–$[maximumPay] for a 3-hour session”

- If pay data is unavailable:
  Show “Select a session to see exact pay”

Format currency consistently to no more than two decimal places.

Examples:
- $75
- $110.58
- $66–$111

Do not show an hourly rate in the hero unless hourly pay is the primary value stored in the session data. Prioritize total estimated pay.
